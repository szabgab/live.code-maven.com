# Why Maven?

Some people hearing the word Maven immediately think about the [Apache Maven](https://maven.apache.org/) project.

We have nothing to do with it.

**Maven** means "an expert" or better yet "an expert who likes to share knowledge".

It originated from the Hebrew word "mevin" (spelled מבין) that means "understand". It arrived to American English via [Yiddish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yiddish)
as explained [here](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maven).

When I heard it the first decade of the 21st century I immediately liked the word. Partially because [my sister is a Yiddish teacher](https://verele.com/).

I started to use it first for my Perl-related content on th [Perl Maven](https://perlmaven.com/) site and then for everything else through the [Code Maven](https://code-maven.com/) site.
