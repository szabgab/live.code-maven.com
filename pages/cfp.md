# CFP - Call for Speakers

If you are interested in giving a presentation, please reach out to [Gabor Szabo](https://szabgab.com/contact). If you don't have a connection to him yet then the best is probably via [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/szabgab/) (include a personal message in your connect request).

We are looking for presentations primarily in the [topics](/topics) we already have, but we won't shy away from other topics either.

## What is needed?

Once we agreed upon a presentation this is what is needed to schedule it:

* A title.
* A few lines of description (teaser) about the topic. (Markdown with links is fine.)
* Expected length in minutes. (Usually 20-60 min, but we can discuss longer sessions.)
* The name of the guest speaker(s).
* A few lines of bio of the speaker(s). (Markdown with links is fine.)
* LinkedIn profile links and/or homepage links.
* Picture of the guest speaker(s). We can usually take this from the LinkedIn profile, but if that's not available or there is a preference to another one then provide that. (aspect ratio: 1:1)

## Format

* 30 min of mingling in which people can introduce themselves. No recording.
* 20-60 min of presentation. People can ask questions via chat. Either the speaker can read or I can read for the speaker. The speaker can answer them right away, or at the end of the pesentation or not at all. This part is recorded and published to YouTube.
* When we are done recording the presentation we have another open mic session with more mingling and informal discussion. No recording.
